LENTIGLASS · 1.0

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device

LentiGlass has no accounts, advertisements, tracking tools, or analytics SDKs. The app does not transmit photos, camera frames, depth, motion, or app preferences to the developer's servers.

Permissions and processing

Camera access is requested when you choose to capture a print. Optional LiDAR data assists with distance on supported devices. The system photo picker supplies only the image you choose. Motion data controls the local viewing angle and optional home-screen light. Optical rendering happens on the device.

Storage, sharing, and deletion

Language, calibration, and lens preferences are stored locally. Export creates a temporary PNG containing optical parameters. The app does not copy source GPS metadata into its generated PNG. Content leaves the app when you choose a destination in system sharing, whose own privacy terms apply. Delete saved exports in Photos or Files. Deleting the app removes its local preferences and temporary files; exported copies remain where you saved them.

Your controls

You can disable camera access in iOS Settings, turn off LiDAR in the camera menu, disable Follow Phone in preview controls, turn off Animated light in home settings, and choose another app language without restarting.

This website and support requests

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider processes standard connection information, such as IP addresses, to serve and secure the website. We do not add analytics or tracking scripts. If you email support, the contact details and information you send are used to respond to your request; you can ask us to delete that correspondence.

Updates and contact

This policy describes version 1.0. If data practices change, this page and the App Store privacy details will be updated before release. Contact: peakmind0@peakmind.com.tw.
