Support

Get started

Choose Try the A/B Flip on the home screen. Tilt left or right, or drag to change the viewing angle. Import an existing interlaced image with Choose Photo, or select Capture a Print to open the camera.

My photo does not flip

The app simulates a lenticular sheet over existing artwork. An ordinary photo contains only one view. To see a flip, use an image that already interlaces two or more views at the corresponding lens pitch and physical size.

View, rotation, and alignment

Dragging the canvas always changes the viewpoint. View, Rotate, and Align select the crescent dial’s target; use the dial or numerical fields to edit lens settings. Pinching zooms around the center.

Density comparison

Open Lens settings > Calibrate to compare nearby LPI values from the left and right. Use Fine-tune this value for 0.01 LPI steps. Apply changes only density; Cancel preserves your settings. A value such as 40.3 can suit the built-in A/B example without being correct for every image, size, or viewing distance. This comparison is not a physical lens measurement.

Camera and focus

Camera access is needed only for capture. Continuous autofocus is enabled; tap the print to move the focus and exposure point. Optional LiDAR assists with distance, but is not required for preview or framing.

Steady preview and energy use

Handheld stabilization filters small tremors and follows deliberate movement. Horizontal motion responds more quickly while keeping stabilization enabled. The image refines when steady. Turn off Follow Phone to hold the angle or enable Energy saving to limit rendering to 15 fps. Rendering pauses after convergence; a moving preview still uses the GPU.

Language and contact

Open Home > Settings or Preview > More options to select English, Traditional Chinese, or System Default. For support, email support@peakmind.com.tw with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce. Please avoid sending personal photos unless needed.
